# Zero Form — brag plan, take 2: problem first

**Angle:** Taipei's company-registration forms ask for the same facts again and again. That's 40 blanks, but only 11 different facts. Retyping invites typos, and staff have to cross-check every form by hand. Zero Form takes each fact once, verified at the source, fills every form from it, and gives staff one verified record to review.

**Hook:** The 7 real forms, filled in. The same facts light up across all of them, colour by colour, until most of every form is a duplicate.

**Who it's for:** Founders filling the forms, *and* the officers reviewing them. The video now ends on the staff side.

**Share caption:** Registering a company in Taipei: 40 blanks across 7 forms, but only 11 different facts. Zero Form asks each fact once, verifies it, and gives staff one record to check.

## Tone and identity
`polished`. Same identity as take 1: paper `#F5F5F2`, ink, accent `#2B50FF`, Inter and Noto Sans TC. It reuses the real `style.css`, `schema.js` (`docBody`, `formBindings`) and the **real officer console** (`officer.html?mock=1`, with mock values aligned to the video's applicant data). All data is the repo's fictional demo data. The typo in scene 2 is illustrative.

## Music
120 BPM, 28s = 14 bars. **D minor** and sparse for the problem (0–10s), then it lifts to **F major** with a soft groove at the reveal. Every cut, highlight, keystroke and check lands on the beat grid.
